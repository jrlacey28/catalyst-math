# Surface integrals

Integrate over surfaces and connect flux with the divergence and Stokes theorems.

Surface integrals use actual surface area; vector flux additionally depends on an oriented normal component.

Scope: A tilted plane's area factor, constant-density mass, and upward versus downward flux.
