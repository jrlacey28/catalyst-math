# Eigenvalues

Find scalars associated with invariant directions and solve characteristic equations.

Eigenvalues are the scalings of nonzero eigenvectors; singularity of A−λI identifies the possible values.

Scope: Diagonal examples, the characteristic equation, and a zero eigenvalue.
