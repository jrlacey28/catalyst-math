# Divisibility and primes

Use the Euclidean algorithm, Bezout identities, and unique factorization.

The Euclidean algorithm preserves common divisors and produces a checkable integer combination.

Scope: Focused first lesson with explicit assumptions and worked examples; not an exhaustive treatment or a substitute for subsequent proofs and independent practice.
