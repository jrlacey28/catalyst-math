# Expectation

Calculate weighted averages, variance, and linearity without requiring independence.

Expectation is a weighted average with linearity, not a prediction of a single outcome or a measure of its risk.

Scope: A finite payoff distribution, linearity, and the difference between expected and individual outcomes.

Four synchronized mathematical scenes, local Kokoro af_heart narration. Written practice is reviewed by a tutor; viewing does not establish mastery.
