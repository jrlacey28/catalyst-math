# Spectral theory

Extend eigenvalue ideas to operators with the required domain and boundedness hypotheses.

In infinite dimensions, spectrum is broader than eigenvalues; spectral-basis theorems require hypotheses.

Scope: Focused first lesson with explicit assumptions and worked examples; not an exhaustive treatment or a substitute for subsequent proofs and independent practice.
