# Improper integrals

Replace unbounded intervals or integrands with limits and test convergence.

An improper integral is a limit of ordinary integrals; small heights or unbounded heights alone do not decide convergence.

Scope: An infinite tail, a divergent comparison, and an integrable endpoint singularity.
