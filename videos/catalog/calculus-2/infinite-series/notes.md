# Infinite series

Use partial sums and convergence tests while distinguishing absolute and conditional convergence.

A series converges when its partial sums converge; term size, signs, and valid error conditions all matter.

Scope: Geometric convergence, harmonic divergence, and a controlled alternating approximation.
