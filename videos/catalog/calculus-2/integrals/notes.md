# Integrals

Connect signed accumulation, Riemann sums, and antiderivatives.

Integrals accumulate signed quantities; distance and other totals may require integrating absolute values.

Scope: Constant and variable accumulation, signed area, and the antiderivative constant.

Four synchronized mathematical scenes, local Kokoro af_heart narration. Written practice is reviewed by a tutor; viewing does not establish mastery.
