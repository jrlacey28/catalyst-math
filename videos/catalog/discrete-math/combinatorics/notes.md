# Combinatorics

Count using sums, products, permutations, combinations, and inclusion-exclusion.

Identify stages, order, and repetition before counting; divide only when you know exactly how many times each outcome was counted.

Scope: The product rule, ordered choices, combinations, and repeated symbols.

Four synchronized mathematical scenes, local Kokoro af_heart narration. Written practice is reviewed by a tutor; viewing does not establish mastery.
