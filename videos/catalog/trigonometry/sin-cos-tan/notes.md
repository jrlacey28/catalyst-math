# Sine, cosine, and tangent

Interpret the three ratios and their reciprocal relationships.

Cosine and sine are coordinates; tangent is their quotient where cosine is nonzero.

Scope: Unit-circle coordinates, exact special values, tangent, and its domain.

Four synchronized mathematical scenes, local Kokoro af_heart narration. Written practice is reviewed by a tutor; viewing does not establish mastery.
