phase_0
The real numbers cannot solve x squared equals negative one. Introduce i with i squared equal to negative one. A complex number a plus b i can be drawn at the point a, b: real part across, imaginary part upward.

phase_1
Add complex numbers component by component. Two plus i, added to one plus two i, gives three plus three i. The arrows in the plane add just like coordinate displacements.

phase_2
Multiplying by i rotates a point ninety degrees counterclockwise about the origin. One becomes i, i becomes negative one, and another two quarter turns return to one. This picture agrees with the powers of i.

phase_3
For general multiplication, distribute and then replace i squared by negative one. One plus i, squared, gives one plus two i plus i squared, which simplifies to two i. Keep real and imaginary terms separate while collecting them.
