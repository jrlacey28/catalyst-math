phase_0
Linear growth adds the same amount each step. Exponential growth multiplies by the same factor. Starting with two and multiplying by one and a half gives two, three, four and a half, and six and three quarters.

phase_1
In a times b to the t, a is the initial value at time zero and b is the per-step factor. A factor of one point five means a fifty percent increase, not a one point five percent increase.

phase_2
A factor between zero and one gives decay. Multiplying by one half repeatedly halves the amount. Zero exponent gives one, so the initial value remains a; negative exponents reverse the multiplication.

phase_3
For a rate r expressed as a decimal, growth uses one plus r and decay uses one minus r. Check the time unit: a monthly rate and a yearly exponent do not belong in the same model without conversion.
