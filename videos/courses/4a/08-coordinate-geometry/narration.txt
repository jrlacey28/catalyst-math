phase_0
Coordinates connect pictures to arithmetic. To move from one point to another, subtract the x coordinates for horizontal change, and subtract the y coordinates for vertical change. Keep each subtraction in the same order.

phase_1
These changes form the legs of a right triangle. The distance between the points is the square root of the sum of their squares. Negative changes are squared, so distance stays nonnegative.

phase_2
The midpoint lies halfway in each coordinate. Average the two x coordinates and average the two y coordinates. Do not average x with y; each axis is handled separately.

phase_3
Slope compares vertical change to horizontal change. Equal slopes show parallel nonvertical lines. A vertical line has zero horizontal change, so its slope is undefined, not zero.
