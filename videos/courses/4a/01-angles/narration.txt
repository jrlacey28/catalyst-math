phase_0
An angle measures a turn, not the length of its arms. A quarter turn is ninety degrees. A half turn makes a straight line and measures one hundred eighty degrees. Watch the ray rotate while the angle changes.

phase_1
These adjacent angles fill a straight turn. If one is fifty five degrees, the other must be one hundred twenty five. Their sum is one hundred eighty. The diagram gives an equation, rather than a number to memorize.

phase_2
When two lines cross, the opposite, or vertical, angles are equal. Each is supplementary to the same neighboring angle. This gives a reason for their equality, not just a visual guess.

phase_3
For two parallel lines cut by a transversal, corresponding angles match, and alternate interior angles match. The parallel condition matters. Without it, similar-looking positions do not guarantee equal angles.
