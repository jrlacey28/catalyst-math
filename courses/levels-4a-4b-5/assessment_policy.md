# Assessment and progression

## Supported practice

Present only three problems at a time and wait for the learner's work. Questions and help are welcome. Default lesson readiness: at least five of the six practice items independently correct, with reasoning on an explanation or application item and every central objective supported. Use judgment when prior evidence already covers an objective; do not demand identical repetitions. This course-specific lesson check replaces the generic four-item lesson gate for these packages.

An assisted answer is useful practice but cannot earn independent credit. Offer a new, genuinely different problem on the affected idea after teaching; do not simply rescore the same answer. Record exact response, item ID, assistance, correctness, reasoning, and confidence if supplied. A missing answer remains unattempted. Do not infer confidence or understanding from silence.

## End test

Use form A after the unit's lessons. Deliver in saved batches of three, without answer keys, hints, or worked examples. Clarifying wording is allowed. Mathematical help pauses independent scoring for that item; teach, then use an unused equivalent B item or a newly written variation. Feedback that reveals a method should be delayed until related independent items have been attempted.

Geometry and Algebra II each have 18 items (36 points). Statistics has 12 items (24 points). Each lesson is sampled by two items. Exact expressions and mathematically equivalent methods are accepted. A calculator is allowed for arithmetic after showing a setup; answer lookup and symbolic solvers do not establish independent readiness.

- **2 points:** correct result and enough reasoning to support the requested conclusion.
- **1 point:** correct core method with a minor arithmetic slip or incomplete justification.
- **0 points:** missing or incorrect central concept, or no assessable response.

An item with substantial assistance is marked assisted and excluded from independent readiness until replaced; it is not silently awarded independent points. Report completed score and pending items separately. A skipped topic does not disappear from the denominator.

## Ready to move on

All of these must hold:

- At least 80% of the possible independent points: **29/36** for 4A or 5, **20/24** for 4B.
- At least one fully correct independent item for every lesson, plus sufficient evidence on each central objective. One item per lesson is a coverage floor, not automatic proof of every subskill.
- Any essential misconception is resolved on a fresh check: examples include area versus perimeter, correspondence in similarity, variance versus standard deviation, AND versus OR in probability, forgotten domain restrictions, omitted square-root branches, or invalid logarithm rules.
- No outstanding prerequisite gap blocks the next topic.

Use the lesson objectives and tutor rubric to explain decisions. This is a tutoring heuristic, not a standardized placement examination. A high score with a conceptual gap triggers focused repair, not a blanket restart. A low score prompts review of the specific weak topics before reassessment.

Use untouched B questions for targeted replacements or a full fresh retest after remediation. B is fresh only until shown; after exposure, author new equivalent questions. Keep all attempt histories and state whether a reported result is the initial test or a repaired readiness result. Do not combine repeated exposure into a misleading first-attempt score.

Record ready_to_advance only from responses. Schedule delayed retrieval about 1–3 days after success and adapt thereafter; label retained only after a delayed independent variation. Video creation, viewing, or a learner clicking Next never raises mastery.

## Next steps by branch

- **4A:** continue supported coordinate geometry or trigonometry after checking algebra prerequisites. Geometry does not require completion of all statistics lessons.
- **4B:** continue conditional probability and statistical inference when arithmetic and algebra prerequisites are secure. Correlation alone never establishes causation.
- **5:** proceed into trigonometry and precalculus according to topic dependencies. Check radians, the unit circle, and function fluency before assuming Level 6 or 7 readiness.

Save learner results under their own student profile. Shared content and media contain no personal scores.
