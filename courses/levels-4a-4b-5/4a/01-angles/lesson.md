# 4A-01 — Angles and intersecting lines

**Goal:** Use a straight angle, vertical angles, and parallel-line angle relationships.

**Prerequisites:** Addition, subtraction, and simple equations.

[Watch the narrated animation](../../../../videos/courses/4a/01-angles/final.mp4) · [Transcript](../../../../videos/courses/4a/01-angles/narration.txt)

Allow 15–30 minutes: watch/pause/replay, attempt [practice set 1](practice-1.md), discuss questions, then attempt [practice set 2](practice-2.md). Time is flexible; move by understanding, not a timer.

Before the next lesson, demonstrate the central objective independently. A useful default is at least 5 of 6 practice items independently correct, including the explanation/transfer item, with any essential misconception repaired on a fresh check. Hints are welcome but require a new independent variation later. The unit-end test checks cumulative readiness.
