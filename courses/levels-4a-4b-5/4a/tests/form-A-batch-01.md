# Level 4A — Test A, set 1

Work independently and show your reasoning. Exact forms are accepted. Ask if wording is unclear; mathematical help pauses independent scoring for that item.

1. **4A-TA-01** A linear pair is (4x+5)° and (2x+25)°. Find x and both angles.

2. **4A-TA-02** Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. An interior angle is 73°. Find its alternate interior angle and name the needed condition.

3. **4A-TA-03** A triangle has angles 35° and 76°. Find the third angle.
