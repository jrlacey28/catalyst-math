# Level 4A — Geometry

9 focused lessons, each with a narrated animation and two practice sets of three. These provide an introductory path through the supplied scope; use targeted extensions when evidence calls for deeper work.

| Lesson | Topic | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Angles and intersecting lines](01-angles/lesson.md) | Use a straight angle, vertical angles, and parallel-line angle relationships. |
| 2 | [Triangles and the Pythagorean theorem](02-triangles/lesson.md) | Use angle sums, triangle inequality, and the right-triangle condition. |
| 3 | [Congruence and rigid motion](03-congruence/lesson.md) | Match vertices and justify triangle congruence with valid criteria. |
| 4 | [Similarity and scale](04-similarity/lesson.md) | Use matching sides, scale factors, and area/volume scaling. |
| 5 | [Circles, arcs, and sectors](05-circles/lesson.md) | Relate radius, circumference, arc length, and sector area. |
| 6 | [Area by cutting and rearranging](06-area/lesson.md) | Derive and use triangle, parallelogram, and trapezoid areas. |
| 7 | [Volume and surface area](07-volume/lesson.md) | Distinguish volume from surface area and use base-area formulas. |
| 8 | [Geometry on the coordinate plane](08-coordinate-geometry/lesson.md) | Use coordinates for distance, midpoint, slope, and geometric claims. |
| 9 | [Proof: connecting claims to reasons](09-proof/lesson.md) | Build a short geometric argument and distinguish evidence from proof. |

End test: 18 questions, two per lesson, delivered three at a time. Tests and a fresh retest form are in tests/. See ../assessment_policy.md for progression decisions.
