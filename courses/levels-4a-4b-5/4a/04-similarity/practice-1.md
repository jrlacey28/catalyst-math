# 4A-04 — Practice set 1

Try these three problems. Show enough working to explain your method. Ask questions if you get stuck; help is welcome during practice.

1. **4A-04-P1** A length of 5 becomes 15 under dilation. Find the scale factor.

2. **4A-04-P2** Similar triangles have matching sides 4 and 10. A second side on the smaller triangle is 6. Find its match.

3. **4A-04-P3** Lengths scale by 3. By what factor does area scale?
