# 4B-06 — Practice set 1

Try these three problems. Show enough working to explain your method. Ask questions if you get stuck; help is welcome during practice.

1. **4B-06-P1** A fair die is rolled. Find P(number greater than 4).

2. **4B-06-P2** A bag has 4 red and 2 blue balls. Without replacement, find P(two red).

3. **4B-06-P3** For that bag, find P(two blue).
