# Level 4B — Statistics Basics

6 focused lessons, each with a narrated animation and two practice sets of three. These provide an introductory path through the supplied scope; use targeted extensions when evidence calls for deeper work.

| Lesson | Topic | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Mean, median, and mode](01-center/lesson.md) | Choose and calculate measures of center; explain outlier sensitivity. |
| 2 | [Variance: squared distance from the mean](02-variance/lesson.md) | Calculate population variance and distinguish it from sample variance. |
| 3 | [Standard deviation and units](03-standard-deviation/lesson.md) | Recover the data’s units and interpret spread without assuming normality. |
| 4 | [Distributions: shape, frequency, and outliers](04-distributions/lesson.md) | Read frequency displays and describe center, spread, skew, and extremes. |
| 5 | [Correlation and its limits](05-correlation/lesson.md) | Interpret direction and linear strength without inferring causation. |
| 6 | [Probability: outcomes and complements](06-probability/lesson.md) | Distinguish both, either, and at least one; use changing counts and complements. |

End test: 12 questions, two per lesson, delivered three at a time. Tests and a fresh retest form are in tests/. See ../assessment_policy.md for progression decisions.
