# Level 5 — Algebra II

9 focused lessons, each with a narrated animation and two practice sets of three. These provide an introductory path through the supplied scope; use targeted extensions when evidence calls for deeper work.

| Lesson | Topic | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Quadratics: completing the square and roots](01-quadratics/lesson.md) | Connect completing the square, vertex form, discriminant, and solving. |
| 2 | [Polynomial functions and multiplicity](02-polynomials/lesson.md) | Read degree, leading behavior, zeros, and repeated factors. |
| 3 | [Rational functions, holes, and asymptotes](03-rational-functions/lesson.md) | Simplify with restrictions, distinguish holes from poles, and check solutions. |
| 4 | [Radicals and extraneous solutions](04-radicals/lesson.md) | Use principal roots, simplify radicals, and check squared equations. |
| 5 | [Complex numbers as points and rotations](05-complex-numbers/lesson.md) | Represent a+bi and perform addition and multiplication with i²=-1. |
| 6 | [Exponential growth and decay](06-exponentials/lesson.md) | Distinguish repeated multiplication from addition and interpret growth factors. |
| 7 | [Logarithms: asking for the exponent](07-logarithms/lesson.md) | Translate exponential/log forms, use log laws with valid domains, and solve. |
| 8 | [Sequences: additive and multiplicative patterns](08-sequences/lesson.md) | Use explicit and recursive rules with correct indexing. |
| 9 | [Series: adding sequences](09-series/lesson.md) | Distinguish terms and sums; use finite sums and basic geometric convergence. |

End test: 18 questions, two per lesson, delivered three at a time. Tests and a fresh retest form are in tests/. See ../assessment_policy.md for progression decisions.
